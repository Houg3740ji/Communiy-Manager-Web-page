// the reality

MOST EXECUTIVES
ARE INVISIBLE
WHERE IT MATTERS.

LinkedIn is the one platform where your target audience — investors, senior partners, potential clients, the press — is actively looking for credible voices in their industry. And most executives are either completely absent, or present in a way that undermines their actual standing.

A dormant profile or generic corporate content doesn't signal "too busy to post." It signals "not leading the conversation." In B2B, that costs deals before the first call.

The question isn't whether to be on LinkedIn. It's whether LinkedIn is working for you or quietly working against you.

// common profile audit findings
GENERIC HEADLINE "CEO at [Company]" tells them nothing about why they should care.
EMPTY ABOUT SECTION Or worse — a third-person bio that reads like a Wikipedia draft.
LAST POST: 2 YEARS AGO Silence reads as irrelevance. Consistency reads as authority.
CORPORATE CONTENT Company announcements and press releases perform ~4× worse than personal insight.
NO CLEAR POSITIONING Visitors can't tell what you stand for or why they should follow you.

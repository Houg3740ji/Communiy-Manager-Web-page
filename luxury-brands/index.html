// The problem with standard social media management

STANDARD PLAYBOOKS
DAMAGE LUXURY BRANDS

Most social media agencies were built to serve mass-market brands: post frequently, optimise for reach, chase trends, measure follower counts. That approach actively contradicts how luxury brands work.

Luxury is built on scarcity, selectivity, and aspiration. A luxury brand that posts twice a day, uses viral formats, and partners with macro-influencers is communicating the opposite of what it should: it's saying it needs your attention rather than deserving it.

The production standard, the frequency, the platform selection, the content strategy, and the success metrics all need to be different for luxury brands. Most agencies don't know how to operate at this level — because their processes were never designed for it.

What luxury brands need is a partner who understands that every visual touchpoint carries the weight of what the brand charges, and who holds that standard without needing to be reminded.

WHAT LUXURY BRAND

// 01 — Strategy

BRAND AUDIT & POSITIONING STRATEGY

Before anything goes live, I audit the current social presence against the brand's positioning: what's working, what's contradicting the brand, what's missing. Then we define the social strategy — platform selection, content pillars, visual identity standards, tone of voice — that aligns with where the brand needs to be.

// 02 — Content

ART-DIRECTED CONTENT CREATION

Content created at the standard a luxury brand demands: art-directed imagery, storyboarded video, crafted copy. Not stock assets, not repurposed content, not templates. Every post is a deliberate brand touchpoint — the visual identity, the caption, the timing — all held to the standard of the brand.

// 03 — Community

SELECTIVE COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT

Managing the brand's social presence with the same selectivity the brand applies to everything else. Responding to qualified interactions, curating mentions, managing DM inquiries, and protecting the brand's comment sections — without over-moderating in a way that kills authentic engagement.

// 04 — Influence

PRECISION INFLUENCER STRATEGY

Identifying and managing partnerships with influencers whose audiences match the brand's target customer — not by follower count, but by audience quality, lifestyle alignment, and genuine brand affinity. For luxury brands, one right partnership is worth more than twenty wrong ones.

// 05 — Reporting

BRAND-ALIGNED PERFORMANCE REPORTING

Monthly reporting focused on the metrics that matter for luxury brands: qualified reach, lead quality, engagement from target demographics, and press mentions driven by social content. Not vanity metrics — data that tells you whether the brand's social presence is building what it should.
